Bao

The mancala of the Swahili coast

Computer 32 seeds To move Winner
You 32 seeds To move Winner

How to play
  1. Your side is the two rows nearest you. Pick one of your holes with at least 2 seeds.
  2. The seeds are sown one per hole around your two rows: left to right along your inner row, then right to left along your outer row.
  3. If the last seed lands in an empty hole, your move ends.
  4. If it lands in a full hole in your inner row and the opponent's hole opposite has seeds, you capture them into your hole, and your move ends.
  5. Otherwise you pick up that hole's seeds and keep sowing.

Empty your opponent's inner row, or leave them with no hole of 2 or more seeds, to win.
