The first time I took a matatu from town to Rongai, I got on the wrong one. Twice.

## How to find the right stage

Every route has a **number** and a **stage**, and the stage matters more. Ask the *makanga* (the conductor), not the driver: the driver is busy, and the makanga knows everything.

- Have small notes ready: KES 50 and 100
- Sit near the door if you're getting off early
- Say "shukisha" when you want to get off

> The loudest matatu isn't always the fastest. But it usually is.

## What it costs

Fares change with the time of day and the rain. A trip that's `KES 80` at noon can be `KES 150` at 6pm in a downpour.
