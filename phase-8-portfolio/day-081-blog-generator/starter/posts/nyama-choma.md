Three Saturdays, four places, one very full stomach.

1. **Mama Njeri's**: the goat ribs, with kachumbari that has actual heat
2. **The Roadside Grill**: slow, smoky, worth the wait
3. **Kwa Baraka**: the ugali alone is worth the trip

Order by weight, not by plate. Half a kilo feeds two hungry people, or one very determined one.
